# Ghost · Autopilot for WhatsApp

Official landing website for **Ghost** (`v1.8.6`) — the 100% on-device Android courier that wakes your phone on schedule, opens WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, types custom `{name}` messages one by one, taps Send, and locks the screen.

- **Live Website:** [https://ghost.justsimpleapps.online](https://ghost.justsimpleapps.online)
- **Android App Repository:** [https://github.com/owaiskhan-ash/Ghost](https://github.com/owaiskhan-ash/Ghost)

## Core Capabilities

1. **01 · Wakes on Cue** — Lights up your Android screen at the exact scheduled minute.
2. **02 · Opens WhatsApp** — Launches WhatsApp or WA Business and injects personalized `{name}` variables.
3. **03 · Sends One by One** — Dispatches messages sequentially at a natural human pace with zero ban risk.
4. **04 · Closes WhatsApp** — Exits back to the home screen and locks the device automatically.
